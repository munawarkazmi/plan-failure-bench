# Citation checks for the related work section

This record was Appendix B of the paper until 24 September 2026, when it moved here so the paper could stay within its page budget. It is kept as written at the time of the checks, apart from the note on item 3.

Every citation in the related work section was checked against public records for its bibliographic details, and every finding attributed to a paper was checked against that paper's body rather than its abstract. This record states what each check established, so a reader can audit the section's claims about prior work the same way they can audit its numbers.

1. Bibliographic details of every entry confirmed against public records; Plancraft's venue upgraded to COLM 2025 and AmbiK's to ACL 2025 in the process.
2. Plancraft's paper body was read; the F1 0.71 figure, the overeager declaration trade-off, and its mechanical scoring are stated in the related work section from the body, not the abstract.
3. Unsolvability recognition under obfuscation is prior art and the related work section says so, with figures confirmed against the paper body (Valmeekam et al. 2024); the specific dissociation we observe, detection surviving obfuscation while execution collapses within one model, remains unlocated in prior work. Note: that dissociation was later retired as a version 1 token artefact (see the obfuscation results in the paper), so the paper no longer claims it.
4. Paired measurement exists within single families and the text credits each instance, AmbiK's counterparts, KnowNo's help budget, Plancraft's overeager declarations, and the false impossibility claims of Valmeekam et al. (2024); our pairing claim is its systematic extension across all six families.
5. As far as searching can resolve it, no embodied refusal benchmark manipulating surface semantics was found, so that claim stands as "to our knowledge".
6. Yes-Man scores with an LLM judge and reports no false positive column on valid instructions, confirmed from the paper body; HomeBench and Semantic Denial of Service are cited; RoboBench is cited as a contrast on judge-free scoring.
7. TREK's feasible versus infeasible split, typed causes, judge-free deterministic scoring, and cause-naming requirement are stated from the paper body, as are AgentAbstain's paired-task construction, paired accuracy definition, and LLM-judge terminal scoring.
8. On the measurement-validity axis, the quantitative claims attributed to the 445-benchmark construct validity review and the 21-judge reliability evaluation, and the design contrasts attributed to SIMMER (all tasks solvable; no refusal, no false positive column, no semantic manipulation), are stated from the paper bodies; the Clever Hans finding is stated in its abstract's own terms.
9. A final check of the entries not individually re-read in earlier rounds confirmed their bibliographic details against public records and their attributed findings against source text: the construct validity review's 445 benchmarks and 29 reviewers, AmbiK's paired ambiguous and unambiguous counterparts for comparing detection methods, HomeBench's valid and invalid instructions with frontier failure concentrated on invalid multi-device cases, SIMMER's world-model executor, RoboBench's multimodal-model-as-world-simulator scoring, and the semantic denial-of-service result that short safety-plausible injected phrases halt execution independently of physical danger.
